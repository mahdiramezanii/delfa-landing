From the South, inspired by the dolphin

We always wanted to be more than a team of programmers: a team that turns ideas into reality, and uses technology, creativity and AI to build things that truly make an impact.

For us, choosing a name was never about finding a pretty word. We wanted a name with identity and roots, one that tells part of our story. Our roots go back to the south of Iran, so we looked to the nature of the south to find ourselves: to the creatures that live beside us and are part of this land’s identity. Among them all, the dolphin caught our attention.

To us, the dolphin was more than a sea creature. What drew us in was its intelligence, its curiosity, its ability to communicate, and the way it engages with its surroundings. It doesn’t rely on strength alone to find its way; it uses its mind, learns, and connects with the world around it. We asked ourselves: isn’t that what we are looking for in technology too? We write code, design systems, work with AI and build digital solutions. But building isn’t all that matters to us. We want to understand, to discover, and to find solutions that make work smarter and simpler.

The name also reminded us of Delphi, the language that, for us, is tied to the world of programming and to memories of technology. That connection led us to combine two ideas: the intelligence of the dolphin and the programming world of Delphi. That is how we arrived at DelFa: a name that is more than a name to us. It is a bond between our roots, our passion for technology, and the way we look toward the future.

DelFa is a team that started in the south, drew inspiration from the dolphin’s intelligence, and chose a path of building with technology, learning, and going one step further every day.

Rooted in the South. Engineered for the Future.
